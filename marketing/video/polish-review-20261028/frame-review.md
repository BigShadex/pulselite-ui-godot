# Final encoded frame review

`final-encoded/` contains twelve frames extracted from the actual corrected MP4s after encoding: title, in-demo layout and closing card for each video. `verification.json` binds the frames to video hashes and timestamps, and records independent technical checks.

Other design stills in this folder are layout checkpoints. The earlier `redo-review-20261027/` folder describes the previous rejected version and is preserved as history.

The corrected files still require GB's full watch-through with sound before creative acceptance. Technical checks and muted playback do not establish listening or permission to upload. The menus are illustrative and not included with the packs; source audio uses procedural DSP and the code/video/text were AI-assisted.
