## What's in the box: 104 Sci-Fi UI sounds

104 distinct sounds across 13 categories, with eight variations per category. Every sound includes a 44.1 kHz, 16-bit mono WAV master and an OGG copy with the same name.

Also included: `index.csv` with per-file descriptions and measurements; JSON/CSV maps for all 14 UI events; Godot and Unity helper scripts; quality-control report, provenance notes and licence. The ZIP also contains the documented Maaack integration scene and UCS rename map.

Per the included provenance notes, the audio is procedurally synthesized from code, without AI-generated audio or recorded sample libraries. Helper scripts, documentation and page copy use AI assistance.
