## What's in the box: PulseLite Complete

408 distinct UI sounds across three included packs: Menu Kit (200), Sci-Fi UI (104) and Horror UI (104). Every sound has a 44.1 kHz, 16-bit mono WAV master and a matching OGG copy: 408 WAV files plus 408 OGG files.

| **Total** | **14 events in 13 categories** | **200** | **104** | **104** | **408** |

Toggle is one category with two events: Menu Kit has eight on and seven off; each themed pack has four on and four off. Across Complete, that is 16 on and 15 off, or 31 toggle sounds in total.

Each pack folder includes its own README, licence, per-file `index.csv`, JSON/CSV event maps, QC report, Godot helper and Unity helper. The bundle contains Menu Kit version 1.2 and Sci-Fi/Horror version 1.0. This is a sound bundle; example game interfaces are not included templates.

Per the supplied provenance, the sounds use procedural DSP rather than AI-generated audio. Helper code, documentation and this draft use AI assistance. See each included licence for its terms.
