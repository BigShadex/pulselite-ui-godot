# Draft storyboard asset credits

The transparent overlays and safe-zone guide are original code-drawn artwork
created with Pillow. They contain no third-party images, no video frames, and
no sound files. Event and file labels are example text copied from the accepted
public `marketing/video/youtube-20261028/cue-manifest.json`; they do not prove a
sound was played. No commercial audio is embedded or rendered in this task.

Typography uses Pillow's built-in Aileron Regular in the installed Pillow
11.1.0 package; no font file is bundled and no Windows font is referenced. The
font author labels Aileron “No Rights Reserved” and allows use, modification,
and redistribution: https://dotcolon.net/fonts/aileron/
