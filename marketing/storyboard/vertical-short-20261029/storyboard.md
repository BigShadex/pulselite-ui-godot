# PulseLite vertical Short storyboard — draft

This is a proposed 25-second portrait edit plan for a future video pass. It is
not a video, a capture, or a guarantee that every platform control overlay will
avoid the content. The separately supplied safe-zone diagram is conservative
guidance; check the destination app's current preview before any publication.

| Proposed time | Picture / pack | Single on-screen cue | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.4 s | Standalone `PULSELITE` title over a neutral portrait game-menu scene | None | Establish the sound-effects hook before the first cue. Opening overlay includes the illustrative-UI and AI/DSP disclosures. |
| 2.4–7.4 s | Menu Kit menu; show a single focused button action | `button_click` → `click_03.ogg` | Proposed event/file label is copied from the accepted public cue manifest; no audio is embedded in these PNGs. |
| 7.4–12.4 s | Sci-Fi settings / panel transition | `panel_open` → `panel_open_04.ogg` | One caption card, with the right and bottom overlay reserve left clear. |
| 12.4–17.4 s | Horror reward screen | `reward` → `reward_05.ogg` | Keep the reward art dominant and show this cue only during its beat. |
| 17.4–21.8 s | Rapid but legible portrait cuts across the three themes | One event/file caption at a time | Optional future edit section; do not stack captions or show a scrolling event list. |
| 21.8–25.0 s | Closing card | `bigshadex.itch.io` / “Links in description” | Include exact product links and the previewer link in future video metadata; keep the end card's not-included disclosure legible. |

The example captions identify source filenames only. This storyboard task does
not include or play any commercial source audio, and does not produce a GIF or
video. The pictured interface should be labelled illustrative and not included
with the sound pack.
